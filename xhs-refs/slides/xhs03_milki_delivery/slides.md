# xhs03_milki_delivery

## 第 1 段

![第1段截图](frame_001.jpg)

Milky Delivery is a short but satisfying adventure game.，You play as Neha, a young bike courier, travelling across beautiful landscapes.，Deliver milk to a bunch of friendly villagers.，Take care of your pet car milky and heal the community.

---

## 第 2 段

![第2段截图](frame_002.jpg)

Upgrade your bike to travel faster and farther to new destinations.

---

## 第 3 段

![第3段截图](frame_003.jpg)

Manage your resources and experiment with different flavors like strawberry or milk tea.，With each delivery, you learn more about the region, its people, and what happened to all the other cows.，The demo is now live on Steam, so if you want to support us, go check it out and wishlist the game.
