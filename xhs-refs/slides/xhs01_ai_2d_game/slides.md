## 第 26 段

![第26段截图](frame_026.jpg)

Click Add Movie，Then import your animation，Next, choose the frame range you want for the loop，Go to Output, Frame Range and change the start and end frames，You want the first and last frames to look similar，So the animation loops smoothly，Press the space bar to preview the animation，Now we'll remove the green screen，Inside the strip modifier settings, add modifier，Compositor，New，Open the Compositor workspace，And enable Sequencer Compositor nodes，Then press Add，Keying，Keying，And place the node between the existing nodes，Click on Key Color，Use the dropper tool and sample the green background，Now your video has transparency，To export the frames, go to Output properties，And adjust the resolution if needed，Choose where you want to save your files，Under Media Type, choose Image，File Format, PNG，Color，RGBA，Then go to Render，Render Animation，Blender will export all frames as transparent PNG images
