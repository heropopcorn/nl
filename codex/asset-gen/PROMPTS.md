# Imagegen final prompt set

All five images used OpenAI's built-in imagegen with the supplied project references. The common locked direction was: warm hand-painted 2D cozy fantasy village, light East Asian influence, non-pixel and non-photorealistic, soft daylight, low-saturation natural palette, no labels or watermark, and genuinely transparent alpha.

## `characters-leads-teachers-sheet.png`

Production-ready transparent character sheet with exactly eight isolated full-body figures in a 4×2 grid: childhood male lead; childhood female lead in ordinary clothes; the same female lead in a slightly refined mist-blue outfit; two distinct male teachers; two distinct female teachers; one neutral classmate. Preserve the supplied V1 faces, hairstyles, proportions, clothing silhouettes, bags and teacher tools. Children are 7–8; teachers are adults. Front or gentle 3/4-front stance, full head-to-shoes framing, equal baselines, generous clear gutters. No scenery, panels, swatches, overlap, weapons, spell effects, luxury court dress or strong palace styling.

References: lead production sheet, teacher candidate sheet, approved terrain asset sheet.
